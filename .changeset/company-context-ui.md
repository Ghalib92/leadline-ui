See what the AI knows about your organization.

- **AI summary tab** (Settings › General › AI summary): the company dossier the AI writes from your profile, preferences and documents — summary, focus areas, track record, past funders, eligibility and search terms — plus a list of what's missing that would improve matching. It updates on its own when those change; admins can regenerate it.
- **Document processing status:** each document shows whether the AI has read it (Queued, Reading, Read by AI, Failed, Not readable), why not when it couldn't, and a Retry button for admins when processing failed. The list refreshes itself while documents are being read.
